Dumbwaiter

A medieval-style lift for moving between vertically-separated floors. Step onto a station, pull the lever, and ride a brief, atmospheric transition — chain rattle, pulley squeal, wood groan, and a fade — to its paired station above or below. Or load a chest, basket, or storage vessel onto the hoist and send your goods up or down without riding along. Perfect for cliff-side bases, multi-floor towers, mineshafts, and deep cellars.

How it works

Place a Dumbwaiter Station at each end of your shaft, link them with a Rope Link, and you have a working lift. Activating one station whisks you to the other through a short immersive sequence whose length scales with the distance traveled.

Linking two stations

  1. Place one station at the bottom of your shaft, one at the top.
  2. Right-click the first station with the Rope Link → "Dumbwaiter anchor set at (x, y, z)." Wrong station? Sneak + right-click anywhere to clear the anchor.
  3. Right-click the second station with the Rope Link → "Dumbwaiter linked! Distance: N blocks."

Both ends now remember each other, and the link item is consumed.

If a link is refused, the message tells you exactly why: which limit you exceeded (vertical distance or horizontal offset), by how much, and the coordinates of the anchor it measured from.

Sending cargo

A true dumbwaiter hauls goods, not just people.

  1. Put a container on top of the station. Stand beside a linked station (not on the platform) and place a chest, basket, or storage vessel on top of the hoist frame — aim at the top of the frame and sneak + right-click to place it, exactly like placing on any other block. If you use CarryOn, you can carry a full vessel over on your shoulder and set it down on the frame the same way (sneak + right-click, empty hands). It can be full — contents come along.
  2. Send it. Still standing beside the station, right-click the station (no sneak). You'll hear the chain rattle and pulley squeal — at the end of it, the container is gone from your end and sitting on top of the station at the other end, with everything inside untouched. Chest labels survive too.
  3. Collect it at the far end. Break it loose, open it in place, or CarryOn it off the frame. The landing spot must be collected before the next delivery can arrive there.

Things that will stop a send: a blocked landing spot at the far end, a walled-off shaft, a trunk (too bulky), or a reinforced/locked container. If anything changes mid-trip — someone breaks the container or fills the landing spot — the mechanism jams safely and the container stays where it was. Nothing is ever lost or duplicated.

Labels

Sneak + right-click a station while holding an ink & quill (reusable) or a piece of charcoal (consumed, with a chance of getting it back — same economy as vanilla labeled chests) to write a label, e.g. "Cellar" or "Side Entrance". The label is shown prominently when you look at the station, and the other end of the link shows it by name — so you always know where the ride goes.

Limits & behavior

  • Max vertical distance: 250 blocks
  • Max horizontal offset: 4 blocks (allows slightly angled shafts)
  • Transition time: ~1–3 seconds, scaling with trip distance
  • Cooldown after arrival: ~0.5–2 seconds, scaling with trip distance
  • To ride, you must be standing on the platform; to send cargo, stand beside the station with a container on top.
  • One container per trip; a delivered container must be collected before the next arrival at that end.
  • The destination must be loaded and unobstructed — the lift will not drop you into a wall.
  • The shaft column between the stations must be clear — walling it off jams the mechanism until cleared.
  • One rider at a time per station (prevents double-activation).

Configurable

A dumbwaiter.json config file (auto-generated in ModConfig) lets you tune max distance, horizontal offset, transition and cooldown timing, sound volume, and an optional toggle for the screen-fade effect.

Compatibility

  • Built for Vintage Story 1.22+
  • Universal (required on client and server); safe for multiplayer.
  • Works with scaled-down player models (Player Model lib races such as the Avali) — small riders can operate the station like anyone else.
  • Plays nicely with CarryOn for loading and unloading cargo.
